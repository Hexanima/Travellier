## Platform

This artifact is a standalone mobile HTML prototype of a React + Capacitor app for iOS and Android. It does not add a public web product.

User-approved plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype, kept separate from the implementation.

Friends and families who plan trips together and create a shared memory of the journey.

## Product Purpose

Coordinate transport and activities and document experiences in posts and photos. This local artifact extends the user's approved original Figma interface in the selected blue palette to the visual scope of PRD v1, sections 3.1–3.8, 6 and 7.

A going choice or new/spontaneous activity detects every existing incompatible going plan.
